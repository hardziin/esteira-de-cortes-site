Esteira de Cortes

Esteira de Cortes is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It helps its owner turn long gameplay videos into short vertical clips and upload those clips to the owner's own accounts (YouTube Shorts and TikTok).

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users. It only posts to accounts that belong to the owner, after the owner reviews and approves each clip.

How it uses TikTok

  • The owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit and authorizes the app.
  • Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's TikTok account as drafts (Content Posting API), where the owner finishes and publishes them in the TikTok app.
