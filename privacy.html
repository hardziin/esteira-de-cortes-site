Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Esteira de Cortes ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What data the app accesses

  • The authorization tokens that the owner grants when signing in with TikTok (and other platforms the owner connects).
  • Basic profile information of the owner's own account (user.info.basic), used only to confirm which account is connected.

How the data is used

Tokens are used only to upload clips that the owner has approved to the owner's own accounts. The app does not access, collect or process data about any other person.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions → Apps), and by deleting the local token files.
